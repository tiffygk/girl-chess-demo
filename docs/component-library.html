Girl Chess: component librarylast updated 2026-09-22

Every component as it ships, grouped by where you meet it in the game. Each one belongs to someone: you, mallow, the coach, or the menu around the board. The archive tab holds the options that lost. The creative direction mixes Adventure Time, Dislyte and Wreck-It Ralph: a retro arcade sugar glitch theme, washed in vaporwave neon and pastel haze. I chose a decidedly feminine and irreverently youthful art style that sets Girl Chess apart from chess, whose default look is masculine, utilitarian and baroque.
