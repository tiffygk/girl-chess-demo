Instrument integrity

Every axis number on this page is measured by a checker whose agreement with hand labels was itself audited. Bars show final checker-vs-hand-label agreement against a 95% threshold.

Checker vs hand-label agreement, per axis (0–100%)
Two audit iterations Two iterations of hand-labeling (473 then 466 rows) against the checker. Iteration 1 found two false-negative bugs: an AI-ism regex matching the verb "lets" instead of only the contraction "let's", and a pending checker missing described-but-not-named moves. Both were patched with regression tests (commits e2d56b8, 0b58498) and confirmed fixed on fresh samples in iteration 2. The length axis's single disagreement is a known, accepted em-dash token-count artifact (1/285).
TakeawayEvery axis's checker agrees with hand labels at 99.6% or better after two audit iterations, so the numbers on this page are measured by a verified instrument, not a trusted script.

Methodology integrity

The controls that make the pick defensible, and the limitations stated in the same breath.

Run order — six runs in ABBA blocks
    Stated honestly ABBA residual imbalance: sonnet ran first in 2 of 3 blocks, so warmup is the primary control and ordering is the belt-and-suspenders. Limitations: 3 reps on one night; subscription-backend latency variance (the same prompt ranged 20s to 80s); the mechanical pick stands in for the owner's blinded read until her morning review.
    TakeawayThree reps per model in counterbalanced blocks, warmed processes, a sha256-guarded database, a pre-registered decision rule, and a blinded artifact waiting for a human read.
